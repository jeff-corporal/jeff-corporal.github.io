Questions people usually ask

Mostly e-commerce sellers and small businesses that want a CPA looking after their books, not just someone entering transactions.

Mostly QuickBooks Online and Xero. I've also worked in NetSuite, SAP and Yardi Voyager, and I'm happy to work in whatever you already use.

It depends on your transaction volume and how many accounts you have. I share pricing after a short call so the quote actually fits your business.

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup work is a common starting point. We get you current first, then keep you there every month.

Yes. Everything is done online, and I keep regular check-ins so you're never wondering what's happening with your books.
